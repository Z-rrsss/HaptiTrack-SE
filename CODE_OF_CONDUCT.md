# Code of conduct

HaptiTrack SE contributors and maintainers must keep project spaces focused,
respectful and free of harassment.

Acceptable participation includes constructive technical feedback, clear
disagreement about code, respect for different experience levels, and care not
to publish another person's private device information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, deliberate
disruption, impersonation and publication of private information are not
acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the project.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately and made
without retaliation.

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private reporting channel.
Do not use a public issue when doing so would expose the affected person.
